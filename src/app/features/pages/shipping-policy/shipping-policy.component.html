Shipping Policy

At ArvinPlus, we strive to deliver your orders promptly and efficiently. This Shipping Policy outlines our shipping practices and procedures.

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day. You will receive a confirmation email once your order is dispatched.

Shipping Rates & Delivery

We offer free shipping on orders above ₹999 within India. Standard shipping rates apply based on your location and order weight. Estimated delivery time is 5–7 business days within India.

Delivery Areas

We currently ship across India, including all major states and union territories. For deliveries to remote locations, delivery time may extend by a few days depending on the courier partner's coverage.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can track your order status using this number on our website or the courier partner's portal.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or with incorrect items, please contact us at support@arvinplus.in within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo of the package. We will arrange a replacement or refund promptly.

Shipping Address

Please ensure your shipping address is accurate and complete. ArvinPlus is not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.
