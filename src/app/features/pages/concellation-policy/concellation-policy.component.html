Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it for a full refund. Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled and will be subject to our return policy.

How to Cancel

To cancel your order, please contact us immediately at support@arvinplus.in or call +91 99906 96316 with your order number. Keep your order number handy to help us process your cancellation faster.

Refund Timeline

Once your cancellation is confirmed, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund is initiated.

Prepaid vs Cash on Delivery

For prepaid orders, the full amount will be refunded to your original payment method after cancellation. For Cash on Delivery orders, no payment is collected until delivery, so no refund is required when the order is cancelled before dispatch.

Contact Us

For any cancellation-related queries, please contact us at support@arvinplus.in or call +91 99906 96316.
